About Us

Meet the passionate team behind LumaWave, combining innovation, engineering expertise, and a shared mission to transform diagnostic imaging.

Dr. Vinoin Devpaul Vincely, PhD is a Founder and Chief Executive Officer of LumaWave, where he leads the development and commercialization of the portable photoacoustic imaging platform for deep-tissue oxygenation assessment. With a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Tulane University and M.S. in Physics at Miami University, Vinoin brings expertise spanning photoacoustic and optical imaging instrumentation, computational modeling, tissue optics, ultrasound physics, signal processing, and medical device translation. He was the co-inventor of LumaWave's photoacoustic platform, taking it from early-stage concept through preclinical validation. As a former innovation fellow, Vinoin has both the technical and entrepreneurial skills to directed product strategy and build commercialization pathways for next-generation imaging technologies.

Dr. Carolyn L. Bayer, PhD is a Founder and the Chief Technical Officer of LumaWave and a tenured Associate Professor of Biomedical Engineering at Tulane University. A leading expert in photoacoustic and ultrasound imaging, she has spent more than a decade developing advanced biomedical imaging technologies for assessing tissue oxygenation, perfusion, and vascular function. Carolyn co-invented LumaWave's photoacoustic platform and has led multiple NIH-funded programs that advanced the technology from fundamental research through preclinical validation. Combining deep scientific expertise with prior industry experience in medical devices, biotechnology, and pharmaceutical development, she provides the technical vision and scientific leadership guiding LumaWave's product development and clinical translation strategy.

Suraj Rajasekhar, PhD is an Optical Systems Engineer at LumaWave, where he applies physics-based modeling and optical imaging expertise to advance the company's photoacoustic imaging technology. Trained through Miami University's Physics and Cellular, Molecular & Structural Biology programs, Suraj brings deep expertise in time-domain diffuse optical spectroscopy, diffusion theory, Monte Carlo light transport simulation, and Julia-based scientific computing. He specializes in turning time-resolved optical data into physically grounded models for deep-tissue characterization, work directly relevant to LumaWave's imaging pipeline. Prior to joining LumaWave, Suraj conducted his doctoral research under Dr. Karthik Vishwanath in the Optical Spectroscopy and Imaging Laboratory (OSIM) in Miami University's Physics department, developing bilayer tissue phantoms and instrumentation to validate optical recovery techniques for biomedical applications.

Keerthi Panguluri, M.S. is an Acoustic Systems Engineer at LumaWave. She earned her master’s degree in Biomedical Engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology and her bachelor’s degree in Bioengineering from the University of California, Riverside. Prior to joining LumaWave, she worked on the design and development of medical devices and assistive technologies, including an open-source near-infrared spectroscopy device for detecting necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), an ostomy refeeding port, and a lateral trunk support system for power wheelchair users. Her work also includes robotic arm development, combining mechanical design, electronics, and programming. She brings experience in circuit design, biomedical signal processing, and hardware integration, with a focus on translating clinical and user needs into functional medical device prototypes.
